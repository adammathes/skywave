## Follow-ups noted in review (2026-10-27)
- Parked-car collision boxes per shape from the built mesh bounds (`world::collide::parked_car_size`
  by shape, not class): the hatch sits in a 4.3 m box, the van and pickup overflow a 1.9 m box.
- Paint as a per-instance attribute so each parked-car shape needs one mesh per LOD (also in
  `docs/IDEAS.md`); today paints are capped at six per shape per place, 27 MB budget test.
- NPC cars do not yet see pedestrians (turning traffic at signals, unsignalised crossings): M4 #6/#8.
- Pedestrians: a dash-display of ICY titles for the native radio needs an `App` method in `ui`.
- Pedestrians: the tests measure a person as a point; in a crowded 6 m/s turn-in 32 person-steps
  came within 0.1 m of the car's outline (worst 0.016 m), half a 0.2 m-wide figure inside it for
  a few frames. Raise `SPOT_CLEAR_M` or keep a body radius clear, and assert that gap.
- Pedestrians: make `Pedestrian::height` public (the app PR needs it to build the figure).
- Pedestrians: chained dodges can carry someone far in one episode (4.47 m seen, each move under
  2 m); bound the episode or walk back between moves.
- Pedestrians: people can stand 12 s on a crossing beside a stopped car; they should finish
  crossing or go back to the curb.
- Pedestrians: `WalkGraph::in_lane` covers the lanes but not the turn curves, so a dodge spot on
  a corner can sit inside a tight turn's path. Adding the curves (16 chords each, as wide as the
  lane they leave) took spots away near corners: the all-crossings sweep went from 0 to 2
  person-steps inside the car, so the spot search needs another look first.
