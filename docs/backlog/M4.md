## Gate facts since the gate
- Player–NPC collision (NOTES 14, `m4/car-collision`): NPC cars are their shapes' body boxes
  with their cars' masses; a hit is a push both ways through `world::collide::hit_car` (the
  wall's impulse with a second body, restitution 0.2; an oncoming car is stopped, a standing one shoved at most 4 m/s), the NPC
  car brakes, stands 1–2 s and steers back to its lane, never shoved off the roadway. The gate's reversing-into-a-queue test
  runs again: the player stops against the queue, nothing overlaps (it was ignored, the player
  passing through).

## Follow-ups noted in review (2026-10-27)
- Parked-car collision boxes per shape from the built mesh bounds (`world::collide::parked_car_size`
  by shape, not class): the hatch sits in a 4.3 m box, the van and pickup overflow a 1.9 m box.
- Paint as a per-instance attribute so each parked-car shape needs one mesh per LOD (also in
  `docs/IDEAS.md`); today paints are capped at six per shape per place, 27 MB budget test.
- NPC cars do not yet see pedestrians (turning traffic at signals, unsignalised crossings): M4 #6/#8.
- Pedestrians: a dash-display of ICY titles for the native radio needs an `App` method in `ui`.
- NPC cars creep at signals rather than standing: after the 20 s warm-up only 1–3 of about 76
  cars stand still at any moment (found while photographing brake lamps for #29). A standing queue
  at a red is what a player expects; worth a car-following look (M4 follow-up).
- Pedestrians: a car teleported onto a group mid-crossing then creeping at 0.2 m/s can reach
  people (the same on main): the walker's escape direction is whichever member was checked last
  (`peds.rs`, `step_walker`), and `along_helps`' `abs()` suppresses that member's own dodge.
- Pedestrians: the vehicle tests call a car stopped under 0.05 m/s, the pedestrians count it as
  moving from 0.05 m/s (`MOVING_MPS`); a braked player car hovering there could flip between the
  two. Settle one threshold.
- Pedestrians: `dodge_target` builds its 50 future car frames eagerly, even when the car goes
  straight and nothing reads them; build them only for a turning car.
