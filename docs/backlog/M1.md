## Acceptance criteria (per issue)
1. `core`: `Pool<T, N>` and `Arena` with no heap growth after construction; tests for exhaustion behavior (returns `None`, never panics); used by nothing yet but documented.
2. `vehicle` specs: loads both RON files; validator rejects non-monotone gears, negative mass, empty torque curve, with specific errors; specs match classes in `docs/VEHICLES.md` roster.
3. `place::test_grid`: ≥ 6×6 blocks, road width 10 m with curbs 0.12 m, buildings 1–8 floors, one `PlayerStart`; `validate` passes; deterministic hash test.
4. CSM: three cascades fit the camera frustum slices, stable under camera rotation (texel snapping), PCF 3×3, no acne at noon or grazing light (screenshot at 3 sun angles); works on the WebGL2 path in CI.
5. Sky: Preetham or Hosek fit evaluated per pixel; sun disc; `Daylight` unit tests for sunrise/sunset times at 34°N on equinox/solstice within 10 min; exposure curve documented.
6. Input: `Bindings` round-trip JSON; table test of raw-event scripts → `InputState` with ramps; conflict resolution function tested.
7. Sim: steps at 120 Hz with no allocation; both cars drive straight hands-off; ordering tests from `docs/VEHICLES.md` pass (initial bands may be wide, tightened in #16); determinism hash test.
8. Loft: both shapes build ≤ 1500 tris, no NaN normals, wheels inside arches; previews attached to PR from 4 angles.
9. Post: HDR Rgba16Float target, 5-level bloom, AgX or ACES tonemap; fog tinted toward the sun; screenshots at dusk and noon attached; WebGL2 OK.
10. Gamepad: standard mapping produces raw events on both platforms; hot-plug test harness native; manual note for web browsers.
11. Collision: scripted drives into a wall at 20/40/60 mph stop without tunneling or bouncing > 0.5 m; curb mounting works at ≤ 30 mph and bumps at higher; auto-right after 3 s inverted.
12. Interior: gauges read live speed/RPM (procedural texture), wheel rotates with steering input, radio quad shows placeholder text; cockpit screenshot attached.
13. Cameras: cycle order and constants per `docs/RENDERING.md`; chase camera never clips into buildings on the test town (scripted path); look-back works.
14. HUD: one bitmap font texture (public-domain font, source credited), text renders crisp at 1080p and 720p; frame-stats overlay toggle.
15. Integration: `?seed=1&car=homestead_wagon` drives on web; native binary drives; scenario test: scripted lap of the test town ends within 5 m of start; no NaNs; frame budget assert.
16. Bands: Sports 0–60 in 5–7 s, Wagon 11–15 s; top speed and skidpad ordering per doc; replay hashes pinned.
17. Screenshots: three M1 scenes golden-captured; diff tool with threshold; CI smoke uses the town overview.
18. Tuning: documented constants and reasoning; a short "how it feels" note in the PR; Pages build link for the gate.

## Out of scope for M1
Procedural world, streaming, time-of-day clock (sun is set by a debug slider), radio, NPCs, settings UI, menus, reflections, native packaging.
