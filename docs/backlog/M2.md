## Acceptance criteria
1. Round-trip encode/decode identical; `validate` rejects each corruption class; single-chunk load reproduces that chunk's meshes bit-identically.
2. Template loads from RON; three templates validate; fields per `docs/WORLD_GENERATION.md` "Region templates".
3. Seed 1 macro map: ocean on the west, a bay, strip/downtown/residential/suburb/hills/farmland/highway zones all present; dump PNG attached; deterministic hash; < 50 ms.
4. 256 lights in view at night on WebGL2 at the frame budget on the CI noop test; screenshots at night show falloff pools under lamps and lit windows.
5. Minimap renders the test town and seed 1; map toggle; TV safe-area margins; screenshot attached.
6. Arterials follow the coast and reach the pass; highway has ≥ 2 ramps and ≥ 1 overpass; ≥ 95% connectivity.
7. Each zone exhibits its pattern in a dump PNG; chunk-border continuity test: generate chunk (x,y) alone and with neighbors → identical border segments.
8. Roads sit on terrain (cut/fill) within ±0.05 m; beach and water render; junction polygons for 3-, 4-, 5-way nodes without overlaps.
9. Parcels cover ≥ 90% of block area; no overlaps; each touches its frontage edge.
10. Coverage targets ± 10% per zone; height distributions per doc; styles vary.
11. Fidelity bar met on seed 1 strip screenshot (curbs, sidewalks, lamps, palms, awnings, neon, parked cars); props in sidewalks never in roads.
12. 9×9 chunk ring at 60 mph: no sim step over 4 ms in a 60 s scripted drive (native release test), pooled buffers never grow.
13. A day passes in 2.4 h at 10×; streetlights on at civil dusk; dusk/night screenshots attached.
14. Scripted drives into a building, a curb, a palm, the ocean behave as `docs/VEHICLES.md` collision section says.
15. Goldens committed with the dump PNGs; any later change must update them deliberately.
16. Four PNGs in `tools/screenshot/golden/`; gate notes in `docs/MILESTONES.md`.

## Out of scope
Radio, NPCs, settings UI beyond time controls, reflections, other regions beyond template stubs.
