# NPCs: traffic and pedestrians

The world should feel inhabited and completely safe. NPCs exist to make a place feel alive in the corner of your eye, not to be interacted with.

## Zero violence, by construction
- Pedestrians are **non-collidable** with the player's car. When a car approaches within 6 m at speed, they step back onto the sidewalk or hop aside; if contact would still occur the car passes through and the pedestrian plays a "whoa" step. No ragdolls, no sounds of impact, no reaction beyond a startled gesture.
- Traffic cars are soft bodies in the sim: they yield, brake and avoid; if the player hits one, the response is a gentle bumper-car push with no damage state, no honking escalation, no chase. A tap of the horn makes them pull aside.
- No police, no wanted level, no NPC speech beyond ambient murmur.
- Settings: traffic density (off/low/normal/busy), pedestrian density (off/low/normal/busy).

## Traffic (`traffic::cars`)
- Vehicles spawn and despawn on lanes outside the player's view frustum beyond 150 m, keeping the density target per resident chunk (normal: 6 per chunk in Downtown, 2 in Residential, 0.3 in Farmland).
- Movement: lane following along `Lanes` from the Place (centerline splines), intelligent-driver-model car following (desired speed = lane `speed_kph` × 0.9–1.1 per driver, gap by speed), stop lines at junctions with a simple right-of-way/traffic-light timer per junction (lights only at arterial junctions; stop signs elsewhere), turn choice weighted toward continuing straight. Lane changes only to pass stopped cars.
- Player interaction: NPCs treat the player's car as a car for following/yielding; they never block a lane for more than 10 s (teleport-despawn when hidden if deadlocked).
- Rendering: the same parametric vehicle meshes, era roster weighted by class popularity, random palette color, headlights on at dusk.
- Sim cost: kinematic (no rigid body), 2D along lanes, ≤ 0.05 ms per car per step; 64 cars resident on web.

## Pedestrians (`traffic::peds`)
- Spawn on sidewalks near the player (40–120 m), despawn beyond 150 m or out of view; density by zone (Strip/Downtown high, Suburb low, Farmland none), time of day (busiest evening on the Strip, few at 4 am).
- Movement: walk along sidewalk polylines at 1.2–1.6 m/s, cross at crossings when the junction light allows, idle in groups near POIs (pier, diner). Kinematic, 2D.
- Appearance: an articulated low-poly figure (600–1200 triangles; as built below), procedural walk cycle, palette by era (70s: earth tones, bell-bottoms, ties; 80s: pastels, shorts, headbands; 20s: athleisure, phones). Variation by proportions, hair block, hat/bag props. No faces beyond a skin-tone block.
- Behavior near the car: described above. Also: wave at a honk 30% of the time.

## Parked cars
Part of the Place detail (`Props::ParkedCar`), static instances from the era roster; collidable like props (soft push).

## Acceptance tests
- Traffic reaches density targets within 30 s at a fixed camera on seed 1; no car intersects another at spawn.
- Scripted scenario: player at 60 mph into a 4-way junction; traffic yields; no NPC position ever overlaps the player hull after the response step.
- Pedestrian scenario: scripted car toward a crowd; every pedestrian ends ≥ 1.5 m from the car's path or passed through; no NaNs.
- Cost: 64 cars + 256 pedestrians step in ≤ 1 ms on the CI runner (release).
- Density settings respected; "off" spawns nothing.

## As built: the lane graph (`traffic::LaneGraph`)
Built once from a Place's road graph (`LaneGraph::new(&place)`, a pure function of the place); `Place::lanes` is empty from every producer today, so the graph is derived, not read. Plain arrays: `lanes`, one `points`/`arc` pool for every centerline, `turns` grouped by the lane they leave, `stop_lines` (`place::StopLine`), one `Junction` per node.
- **Lanes** run junction to junction. Edges are chained through two-road nodes where the road carries on unchanged (same lanes each way, class, width, speed, one-way; bend under 60°), so chunk-border splits never end a lane; `Lane::edge`/`forward` name the edge a lane ends on, `start_node`/`end_node` its nodes. Centerlines are the chained profile (samples ≥ 1 m apart) offset right of travel, at the lane centers the paint uses: two-way roads split each half of the curb-to-curb width less the 0.45 m gutter; highways 3.6 m lanes out from a 1.2 m half median; one-way ramps across the whole width, slot 0 left. Polylines, mitered at bends.
- **Setbacks**: each road end at a junction is cut back to where its pavement clears every other road's (`(hw_other + hw·|cos|)/max(sin, 0.5)`, at most 20 m) plus 4.4 m (crosswalk and stop bar room; 1 m at a bend). A road shorter than its two setbacks keeps a fifth of its length as lane.
- **Turns**: from every incoming lane to the outgoing lanes of every other road at the node. Through moves (within 35°) keep their slot, clamped; left turns go slot 0 to slot 0; right turns outermost to outermost; no U-turns (over 150°) at three-road or larger nodes; nothing over 60° at a node touching a highway (no crossing the median). A lane no rule serves takes the straightest move. A lane into a dead end (`Control::DeadEnd`, degree-1 node) U-turns onto the road's other side; only one-way dead ends trap a car. About 99% of lanes in every region and seed form one strongly connected component; the rest are on road islands. The curve is a cubic from the lane's end to the next lane's start, tangent to both: circular-arc handles for the angle turned, or the quadratic through the corner where the two lane lines meet ahead of both, whichever is rounder. `Turn::length_m` and `min_radius_m` (for turn speed) are measured over 16 chords. Coast seed 1: 2.9% of turns are tighter than 3 m, all on squeezed roads or acute corners.
- **Control is what the world draws.** The place's `StopSign` and `Signal` props (stood by the detailing pass, which skips corners, asphalt and buildings, so not every approach its rule wants gets one) are read back: a stop sign controls the approach whose drivers it faces and stands on their right; a signal pole anywhere at a node signals every approach there (alleys too, which get no pole). Everything else is free. A controlled lane ends at its stop line (`stop_distance(lane, s)`); a signed approach's stop line is the bar `world` paints, 3.85 m past a stop sign and 3.65 m past a signal pole along the road end (within 0.5 m of the paint on 99.8% of approaches, at most about 1 m on bends). On a road too short for both setbacks the unsigned end gives way first; two bars may leave as little as 1 m of lane.
- **Signals** (`traffic::signal`): a fixed cycle of the junction's phases in 120 Hz ticks: 25 s green, 3.5 s yellow, 1.5 s all red each (two phases: 60 s). Phases group approaches by their direction of travel at the stop line: the most nearly opposite pairs (at least 145° apart) share one, every other approach gets its own, so crossing streams never get green together (up to 3 phases on the test places). Each junction's start offset is `hash64(place seed, node) % cycle`. The light is a pure function of the tick (`LaneGraph::light(lane, tick)`, `SignalClock::step`), so all signal state is one counter.
- **Queries** (no allocation): `pose(lane, s)`, `end_pose(lane)`, `turn_pose(turn, s)` (position, heading in the vehicle sim's convention, ground direction), `next(lane)` (the turns), `stop_distance`, `light`.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/tests/places.rs`, all three regions at seeds 1–3): every lane has a turn leaving from its end node but one-way dead ends, and at least 95% of lanes are strongly connected; turn curves meet both lanes in position and direction with no jumps and sharp turns stay under 5%; no two crossing approaches are ever green at once; every stop sign drawn stops a lane by it (or faces a road with no lanes in), every stop lane has a drawn sign and every signal a drawn pole; stop lines sit on the painted bar (`world::roads::Network`). On the coast at seed 1: pinned `LaneGraph::hash` (moves in its own commit, with the reason), `pose` is arc length along the lane, `stop_distance` reaches the stop line, and the graph is under twice seed 1's size.

## As built: the pedestrian figure (`traffic::figure`)
One mesh set per figure, posed by rigid transforms: `figure(seed, era)` (= `build(&FigureSpec::from_seed(seed, era))`) gives a `Figure` with one `vehicle::shape::MeshData` per `Part` (the type `app` already uploads for cars) and the `Skeleton` that poses them. Posing is pure and allocates nothing: `Skeleton::walk`/`step_aside`/`hop` return `Joints` (the pelvis position and each part's `[pitch, yaw, roll]` relative to its parent, `Ry·Rz·Rx`), `Joints::blend` mixes two, and `Skeleton::pose(&joints, &mut [Xform; PARTS])` gives each part's transform in the figure frame (`p' = rot·p + pos`, rows), to draw as one instance per part.
- **Parts** (13, `PARENT` order): pelvis (root, at the hip joints), torso (waist), head (neck base; neck, head and hair), upper and lower arms (hand on the lower), upper and lower legs, and feet on an ankle joint. The feet are their own parts because a foot rigid with the shin cannot stay flat through a stance: with an ankle, the footprint stays planted and rolls heel to toe. Figure frame as the car's: `+x` right, `+y` up, `-z` forward, origin on the ground between the feet.
- **Spec from a seed** (`skywave_core::Rng`, key `PEDS` and the era): height 1.5–1.95 m (mean of two draws), build (slim to heavy: trunk and limb girth, belly), shoulder breadth (against hip breadth), slouch; skin from ten tones light to dark, hair (dark hair mostly with dark skin, grey one time in eight); outfit by era weights: shorts and tee (sneakers, tube socks with a stripe; seventies tees with a sleeve band), sundress (skirt flared from the pelvis, sandals), trousers and an open jacket over a shirt (seventies denim flares to a bell), suit (a tie in the seventies, a pastel tee under it in the eighties), beach (tank top, trunks, sandals); hair cropped, short, long (framing the face, to the shoulders), full, or none; headbands (eighties, casual), sunglasses (often at the beach). Palettes are invented era colors; cloth is kept at least 70/255 (sRGB distance) off the skin. No logos, text or faces beyond a nose.
- **Proportions** (in heights `h`): hip joint 0.51, knee 0.285, ankle 0.04 above the sole, waist 0.61, shoulders 0.81 at ±0.1 (with breadth and build), neck base 0.836; the head is 0.23 m chin to crown at 1.75 m, scaled by √(h / 1.75) so shorter people have relatively bigger heads; foot 0.143 long.
- **Meshes**: smooth lofts of elliptical rings (6–10 sides) with fan caps; clothing is bands over the rings, each cloth edge a pair of rings stepped 5 mm (cuffs, hems, sleeve ends) so color edges are crisp and cloth reads as a layer; a shirt front and tie are thin panels on the jacket, the hair a shell over the skull down to a hairline high at the front, at the jaw (long hair) or ears at the sides and lowest behind. Left limbs mirror the right. 925–1161 triangles over 600 seeds in both eras.
- **Walk** (`walk(phase, speed)`, period 1 in phase; `phase_rate(speed)` is cycles per second, `stride_m(speed)` one cycle's length, `h · (0.42 + 0.23 v)`): left heel strike at phase 0, right at 0.5, each foot down 62% of the cycle. Footprints move straight back under the body while down (planted in the world), then swing forward with a lift; the foot pitches toe up at heel strike, flat, heel up to toe off, back through the swing, rolling about its heel or toe so the contact stays put. Legs are two-bone IK to the ankle (knee forward; the distance eases toward full reach instead of clamping, so the knee never snaps straight), the foot joint keeps the foot's pitch and level. The pelvis rides as high as the planted legs reach, so it dips at double support and rises mid-stance by itself, and sways over the standing foot; hips yaw with the stride and shoulders counter-rotate; arms swing opposite the legs with more elbow on the forward swing; the torso leans with speed on top of the figure's slouch, the head stays level. Below 0.7 m/s the stride fades into standing, and at 0 the same function is an idle sway (weight shift, breathing arms, a slow look around) at `IDLE_PERIOD_S`.
- **Getting out of the way** (poses only; behaviour is the next task): `step_aside(t, side, look)` is a sidestep of `ASIDE_M` (lead foot then trail foot, a small crouch, a lean, a startled lift of the hands that settles) with the head and torso turned toward `look` (`look_yaw(dx, dz)` toward the car); `hop(t, side)` is a crouch, a 0.12 h hop with tucked feet and swinging arms, and a landing, carrying `HOP_M`. Both start (and the hop ends) at the standing pose; the behaviour moves the figure by `aside_offset(t)` / `hop_offset(t)` so planted feet stay put.
- **Preview**: `cargo run -p shape-preview -- --figures out.png [--seed S]` draws twelve figures mid-stride, seeds `S..S+6` in the seventies above and the eighties below (from 126, the default, the twelve wear every outfit in both eras). The sheet as built: `docs/images/pedestrian-figures.png`.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/src/figure.rs`, `crates/traffic/tests/figure.rs`): 600–1200 triangles and sane meshes for 300 seeds per era, with every outfit drawn; the same seed gives equal figures and the pinned mesh hash for seed 1 in both eras (moves only on purpose, in its own commit); no joint moves more than 0.03 rad between phases 1/2000 apart across two cycles and the wrap, at speeds 0–1.6; over a cycle at every speed, no foot goes 2 cm below the ground and one is always within 2 cm of it; arms swing opposite the legs, wider with speed, and the pelvis bobs; step aside and hop stay finite and above ground, start standing, hop clears the ground, the step reaches out and looks at the car; eras dress differently; posing (walk, step aside, hop, blend, pose) allocates nothing.
## As built: cars (`traffic::Traffic`, `traffic::cars`)
Simulation only; drawing, the density setting and sound are M4 #6. `Traffic::new(&graph, TrafficBudget, seed)` builds a fixed pool (default 96 cars) and precomputes which turns conflict; nothing allocates after it (clones keep the reserved pool). Each 120 Hz tick the caller runs `step(&graph, &inputs)` then `populate(&graph, &inputs)`; `Inputs` carries the signal tick, the player (position, heading, speed, half extents), the camera as a horizontal view cone, the clock (seconds of day, dusk and dawn as the streetlights switch) and the density. `cars()` is the live set as a plain slice of `Car`: id (stable for its life), ground position under the middle, heading (vehicle convention) and unit direction, speed, shape index (below `TrafficBudget::shapes`), paint seed, head lights; then the sim state (lane or turn, front-bumper distance `s`, chosen turn, claims).
- **Kinematic, along the graph.** A car is a front-bumper distance on a lane or a turn (`seg`: a lane id, or the lane count plus a turn id), 4.6 × 1.8 m. Its pose is the midpoint of its front and rear points on the path (the rear may still be on the previous segment), heading from rear to front, so it swings through turns.
- **Following:** the intelligent-driver model (1.4 m/s² up, 2.5 comfortable, 8 emergency; 2 m jam gap; time gap 1.1–1.6 s per driver) against the nearest obstacle along the path it will drive (current lane or turn, the chosen turn, the lane after, up to 40–90 m ahead by speed): the car ahead, a closed stop line, or the player found within a car's width of the path. Desired speed is the lane's posted speed (its road class) × 0.9–1.1 per driver; turns cap it at √(2.2 m/s² × min radius), at least 3 m/s, and cars brake ahead for them. A hard limit under the model: a step never moves a car's front closer than 0.3 m to the car ahead (0.6 m to the player) or past a closed line. Under that a backstop: a move that would put a car's body in contact with another car's or the player's, or deeper into one, is not made; roads that cross without a junction (a few in the lakes and suburb places) are what it catches. The player is never hit by construction: a car stopping dead in front of a queue stops it, and cars only ever stand behind.
- **Junctions by claims.** A car must claim its turn to leave its lane, and claims it within its comfortable stopping distance plus 3 m when the gate is open: green (only if it reaches the line 0.5 s before red, gathering speed at half the maximum), yellow when it is too close to stop and still makes it; a stop sign stopped at (under 0.3 m/s within 2.5 m) and waited out (0.5–1.5 s, seeded); room past the junction for its length; no conflicting turn claimed; nobody with the right of way about to arrive; the player not within 14 m of the junction or due there within 4 s. A claim is held through the turn and until the rear has left it; on red a claim is given back and the car stops at the line, so no car enters on red. Two turns conflict when what a car sweeps on them (the curve and the first 5.6 m of the lane after) comes within 2.2 m of the other's or they merge into one lane; turns of nearby junctions are compared too. Turns from one lane share their mouth: cars on them follow each other there and conflict only where the curves run close beyond it.
- **Right of way** among conflicting cars within 5 s of their lines (head of each approach): uncontrolled and green before stop signs; straight and right before left and U-turns; then the earlier arrival (stop signs: the earlier stop); then the lower id. A strict order, so no two cars wait on each other. Red approaches do not contend.
- **Turn choice** when a car enters a turn (or spawns): over the turns from every lane of the road and direction it lands on, weighted straight 6, left or right 2, U-turn 0.25, twice for its own lane. A turn from a parallel lane is reached by changing lanes; within 60 m of the end a car that has not made it picks again from its own lane. Lanes with no turns (one-way dead ends) end at a closed line.
- **Lane changes** (parallel lanes of one road and direction): to the lane the turn leaves from, or to pass a car slower than 85% of its desired speed within 40 m when going straight. A car considers one every 0.25 s, at most one changes per step, never within 60 m of the lane's end or while it holds a claim; it needs 3 m + 0.6 s of speed ahead and behind on the target lane (more when closing). Its new position is the nearest point on the target lane; the sideways jump is eased out at 1.4 m/s, and while it lasts the car still blocks the lane it left.
- **Population.** `Density` off/low/normal/busy keeps 0, ¼, ½ or all of the pool. Spawns: at most one per step, scanning 48 lanes per step round the graph for a point 150–300 m from the player, on a lane flowing toward the player, out of the view cone (with a car's margin), 12 m clear of every car and 25 m of any on its lane, at up to 80% of the posted speed and no faster than it can stop for what is ahead. Despawns, only out of view: beyond 360 m, hidden for 15 s and over 75 m away, stuck (stopped 90 s, longer than any red), or over the density target (beyond 150 m). Head lights come on 0–15 min after dusk per driver and go off as long after dawn.
- **Determinism:** one PCG stream (`skywave_core::Rng`, keyed by the seed) drawn in a fixed order; only IEEE add, multiply, divide and the crate's own `sqrt`/`atan2`/`sin_cos`; cars planned and moved in pool order. `Traffic::hash` is every car, bit for bit.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/tests/cars.rs`): a 12-car platoon on a straight lane settles to at least 2 m + 0.9 s of speed with no contact over 60 s; on a signalized crossing every car that reaches the line on red stops, every car goes through on green, none on red, and the crossing streams never touch; the player stopping dead ahead of six cars is never touched and the queue stops behind; busy traffic on the coast at seed 1 for 120 s (camera turning): nobody enters a junction on red, no two cars on one lane or turn closer than a car length, no contact between cars or with the player standing on a lane, no car appears or vanishes in view, 92–96 cars of 96; every region at seeds 1–3 drives a minute busy with no contact and no red run; low and normal hold 90% of their targets after 30 s, off spawns nothing; pinned `Traffic::hash` after 30 s on the coast at seed 1 (moves in its own commit, with the reason); no allocation in 20 s of stepping; 96 cars step and populate in under 0.5 ms p99 in a debug build (about 0.3 ms measured; asserted under `SKYWAVE_ENFORCE_BUDGETS`, the best of three 5 s windows, with the other tests in the file held off).
