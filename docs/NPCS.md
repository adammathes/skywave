# NPCs: traffic and pedestrians

The world should feel inhabited and completely safe. NPCs exist to make a place feel alive in the corner of your eye, not to be interacted with.

## Zero violence, by construction
- Pedestrians are **non-collidable** with the player's car. When a car approaches within 6 m at speed, they step back onto the sidewalk or hop aside; if contact would still occur the car passes through and the pedestrian plays a "whoa" step. No ragdolls, no sounds of impact, no reaction beyond a startled gesture.
- Traffic cars are soft bodies in the sim: they yield, brake and avoid; if the player hits one, the response is a bumper-car push both ways (the player's car bumps and slows, the NPC car is shoved, brakes, waits a moment and drives on) with no damage state, no deformation, no honking escalation, no chase. A tap of the horn makes them pull aside.
- No police, no wanted level, no NPC speech beyond ambient murmur.
- Settings: traffic density (off/low/normal/busy), pedestrian density (off/low/normal/busy).

## Traffic (`traffic::cars`)
- Vehicles spawn and despawn on lanes outside the player's view frustum beyond 150 m, keeping the density target per resident chunk (normal: 6 per chunk in Downtown, 2 in Residential, 0.3 in Farmland).
- Movement: lane following along `Lanes` from the Place (centerline splines), intelligent-driver-model car following (desired speed = lane `speed_kph` × 0.9–1.1 per driver, gap by speed), stop lines at junctions with a simple right-of-way/traffic-light timer per junction (lights only at arterial junctions; stop signs elsewhere), turn choice weighted toward continuing straight. Lane changes only to pass stopped cars.
- Player interaction: NPCs treat the player's car as a car for following/yielding; they never block a lane for more than 10 s (teleport-despawn when hidden if deadlocked).
- Rendering: the same parametric vehicle meshes, era roster weighted by class popularity, random palette color, headlights on at dusk.
- Sim cost: kinematic (no rigid body), 2D along lanes, ≤ 0.05 ms per car per step; 64 cars resident on web.

## Pedestrians (`traffic::peds`)
- Spawn on sidewalks near the player (40–120 m), despawn beyond 150 m or out of view; density by zone (Strip/Downtown high, Suburb low, Farmland none), time of day (busiest evening on the Strip, few at 4 am).
- Movement: walk along sidewalk polylines at 1.2–1.6 m/s, cross at crossings when the junction light allows, idle in groups near POIs (pier, diner). Kinematic, 2D.
- Appearance: an articulated low-poly figure (600–1200 triangles; as built below), procedural walk cycle, palette by era (70s: earth tones, bell-bottoms, ties; 80s: pastels, shorts, headbands; 20s: athleisure, phones). Variation by proportions, hair block, hat/bag props. No faces beyond a skin-tone block.
- Behavior near the car: described above. Also: wave at a honk 30% of the time.

## Parked cars
Part of the Place detail (`Props::ParkedCar`), static instances from the era roster; collidable like props (soft push).

## Acceptance tests
- Traffic reaches density targets within 30 s at a fixed camera on seed 1; no car intersects another at spawn.
- Scripted scenario: player at 60 mph into a 4-way junction; traffic yields; no NPC position ever overlaps the player hull after the response step.
- Pedestrian scenario: scripted car toward a crowd; every pedestrian ends ≥ 1.5 m from the car's path or passed through; no NaNs.
- Cost: 64 cars + 256 pedestrians step in ≤ 1 ms on the CI runner (release).
- Density settings respected; "off" spawns nothing.

## As built: the lane graph (`traffic::LaneGraph`)
Built once from a Place's road graph (`LaneGraph::new(&place)`, a pure function of the place); `Place::lanes` is empty from every producer today, so the graph is derived, not read. Plain arrays: `lanes`, one `points`/`arc` pool for every centerline, `turns` grouped by the lane they leave, `stop_lines` (`place::StopLine`), one `Junction` per node.
- **Lanes** run junction to junction. Edges are chained through two-road nodes where the road carries on unchanged (same lanes each way, class, width, speed, one-way; bend under 60°), so chunk-border splits never end a lane; `Lane::edge`/`forward` name the edge a lane ends on, `start_node`/`end_node` its nodes. Centerlines are the chained profile (samples ≥ 1 m apart) offset right of travel, at the lane centers the paint uses: two-way roads split each half of the curb-to-curb width less the 0.45 m gutter; highways 3.6 m lanes out from a 1.2 m half median; one-way ramps across the whole width, slot 0 left. Polylines, mitered at bends.
- **Setbacks**: each road end at a junction is cut back to where its pavement clears every other road's (`(hw_other + hw·|cos|)/max(sin, 0.5)`, at most 20 m) plus 4.4 m (crosswalk and stop bar room; 1 m at a bend). A road shorter than its two setbacks keeps a fifth of its length as lane.
- **Turns**: from every incoming lane to the outgoing lanes of every other road at the node. Through moves (within 35°) keep their slot, clamped; left turns go slot 0 to slot 0; right turns outermost to outermost; no U-turns (over 150°) at three-road or larger nodes; nothing over 60° at a node touching a highway (no crossing the median). A lane no rule serves takes the straightest move. A lane into a dead end (`Control::DeadEnd`, degree-1 node) U-turns onto the road's other side; only one-way dead ends trap a car. About 99% of lanes in every region and seed form one strongly connected component; the rest are on road islands. The curve is a cubic from the lane's end to the next lane's start, tangent to both: circular-arc handles for the angle turned, or the quadratic through the corner where the two lane lines meet ahead of both, whichever is rounder. `Turn::length_m` and `min_radius_m` (for turn speed) are measured over 16 chords. Coast seed 1: 2.9% of turns are tighter than 3 m, all on squeezed roads or acute corners.
- **Control is what the world draws.** The place's `StopSign` and `Signal` props (stood by the detailing pass, which skips corners, asphalt and buildings, so not every approach its rule wants gets one) are read back: a stop sign controls the approach whose drivers it faces and stands on their right; a signal pole anywhere at a node signals every approach there (alleys too, which get no pole). Everything else is free. A controlled lane ends at its stop line (`stop_distance(lane, s)`); a signed approach's stop line is the bar `world` paints, 3.85 m past a stop sign and 3.65 m past a signal pole along the road end (within 0.5 m of the paint on 99.8% of approaches, at most about 1 m on bends). On a road too short for both setbacks the unsigned end gives way first; two bars may leave as little as 1 m of lane.
- **Signals** (`traffic::signal`): a fixed cycle of the junction's phases in 120 Hz ticks: 25 s green, 3.5 s yellow, 1.5 s all red each (two phases: 60 s). Phases group approaches by their direction of travel at the stop line: the most nearly opposite pairs (at least 145° apart) share one, every other approach gets its own, so crossing streams never get green together (up to 3 phases on the test places). Each junction's start offset is `hash64(place seed, node) % cycle`. The light is a pure function of the tick (`LaneGraph::light(lane, tick)`, `SignalClock::step`), so all signal state is one counter.
- **Queries** (no allocation): `pose(lane, s)`, `end_pose(lane)`, `turn_pose(turn, s)` (position, heading in the vehicle sim's convention, ground direction), `next(lane)` (the turns), `stop_distance`, `light`.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/tests/places.rs`, all three regions at seeds 1–3): every lane has a turn leaving from its end node but one-way dead ends, and at least 95% of lanes are strongly connected; turn curves meet both lanes in position and direction with no jumps and sharp turns stay under 5%; no two crossing approaches are ever green at once; every stop sign drawn stops a lane by it (or faces a road with no lanes in), every stop lane has a drawn sign and every signal a drawn pole; stop lines sit on the painted bar (`world::roads::Network`). On the coast at seed 1: pinned `LaneGraph::hash` (moves in its own commit, with the reason), `pose` is arc length along the lane, `stop_distance` reaches the stop line, and the graph is under twice seed 1's size.

## As built: the pedestrian figure (`traffic::figure`)
One mesh set per figure, posed by rigid transforms: `figure(seed, era)` (= `build(&FigureSpec::from_seed(seed, era))`) gives a `Figure` with one `vehicle::shape::MeshData` per `Part` (the type `app` already uploads for cars) and the `Skeleton` that poses them. Posing is pure and allocates nothing: `Skeleton::walk`/`step_aside`/`hop` return `Joints` (the pelvis position and each part's `[pitch, yaw, roll]` relative to its parent, `Ry·Rz·Rx`), `Joints::blend` mixes two, and `Skeleton::pose(&joints, &mut [Xform; PARTS])` gives each part's transform in the figure frame (`p' = rot·p + pos`, rows), to draw as one instance per part.
- **Parts** (13, `PARENT` order): pelvis (root, at the hip joints), torso (waist), head (neck base; neck, head and hair), upper and lower arms (hand on the lower), upper and lower legs, and feet on an ankle joint. The feet are their own parts because a foot rigid with the shin cannot stay flat through a stance: with an ankle, the footprint stays planted and rolls heel to toe. Figure frame as the car's: `+x` right, `+y` up, `-z` forward, origin on the ground between the feet.
- **Spec from a seed** (`skywave_core::Rng`, key `PEDS` and the era): height 1.5–1.95 m (mean of two draws), build (slim to heavy: trunk and limb girth, belly), shoulder breadth (against hip breadth), slouch; skin from ten tones light to dark, hair (dark hair mostly with dark skin, grey one time in eight); outfit by era weights: shorts and tee (sneakers, tube socks with a stripe; seventies tees with a sleeve band), sundress (skirt flared from the pelvis, sandals), trousers and an open jacket over a shirt (seventies denim flares to a bell), suit (a tie in the seventies, a pastel tee under it in the eighties), beach (tank top, trunks, sandals); hair cropped, short, long (framing the face, to the shoulders), full, or none; headbands (eighties, casual), sunglasses (often at the beach). Palettes are invented era colors; cloth is kept at least 70/255 (sRGB distance) off the skin. No logos, text or faces beyond a nose.
- **Proportions** (in heights `h`): hip joint 0.51, knee 0.285, ankle 0.04 above the sole, waist 0.61, shoulders 0.81 at ±0.1 (with breadth and build), neck base 0.836; the head is 0.23 m chin to crown at 1.75 m, scaled by √(h / 1.75) so shorter people have relatively bigger heads; foot 0.143 long.
- **Meshes**: smooth lofts of elliptical rings (6–10 sides) with fan caps; clothing is bands over the rings, each cloth edge a pair of rings stepped 5 mm (cuffs, hems, sleeve ends) so color edges are crisp and cloth reads as a layer; a shirt front and tie are thin panels on the jacket, the hair a shell over the skull down to a hairline high at the front, at the jaw (long hair) or ears at the sides and lowest behind. Left limbs mirror the right. 925–1161 triangles over 600 seeds in both eras.
- **Walk** (`walk(phase, speed)`, period 1 in phase; `phase_rate(speed)` is cycles per second, `stride_m(speed)` one cycle's length, `h · (0.42 + 0.23 v)`): left heel strike at phase 0, right at 0.5, each foot down 62% of the cycle. Footprints move straight back under the body while down (planted in the world), then swing forward with a lift; the foot pitches toe up at heel strike, flat, heel up to toe off, back through the swing, rolling about its heel or toe so the contact stays put. Legs are two-bone IK to the ankle (knee forward; the distance eases toward full reach instead of clamping, so the knee never snaps straight), the foot joint keeps the foot's pitch and level. The pelvis rides as high as the planted legs reach, so it dips at double support and rises mid-stance by itself, and sways over the standing foot; hips yaw with the stride and shoulders counter-rotate; arms swing opposite the legs with more elbow on the forward swing; the torso leans with speed on top of the figure's slouch, the head stays level. Below 0.7 m/s the stride fades into standing, and at 0 the same function is an idle sway (weight shift, breathing arms, a slow look around) at `IDLE_PERIOD_S`.
- **Getting out of the way** (poses; the behaviour is `traffic::peds`, below): `step_aside(t, side, look)` is a sidestep of `ASIDE_M` (lead foot then trail foot, a small crouch, a lean, a startled lift of the hands that settles) with the head and torso turned toward `look` (`look_yaw(dx, dz)` toward the car); `hop(t, side)` is a crouch, a 0.12 h hop with tucked feet and swinging arms, and a landing, carrying `HOP_M`. Both start (and the hop ends) at the standing pose; the behaviour moves the figure by `aside_offset(t)` / `hop_offset(t)` so planted feet stay put.
- **Sitting**: `sit(t)` lowers the figure from standing onto a bench seat `SEAT_M` (0.46 m, the place's benches) high, the pelvis going back and down with a forward lean on the way and the hands coming to the thighs; its origin is the seat point, and at `t = 0` it stands `sit_reach(h)` ahead of it on feet that stay planted, so the behaviour switches the origin from the feet to the seat as sitting starts. `phase_rate(h, v)` is `Skeleton::phase_rate` from the height alone, for a behaviour that has not built the figure.
- **Preview**: `cargo run -p shape-preview -- --figures out.png [--seed S]` draws twelve figures mid-stride, seeds `S..S+6` in the seventies above and the eighties below (from 126, the default, the twelve wear every outfit in both eras). The sheet as built: `docs/images/pedestrian-figures.png`.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/src/figure.rs`, `crates/traffic/tests/figure.rs`): 600–1200 triangles and sane meshes for 300 seeds per era, with every outfit drawn; the same seed gives equal figures and the pinned mesh hash for seed 1 in both eras (moves only on purpose, in its own commit); no joint moves more than 0.03 rad between phases 1/2000 apart across two cycles and the wrap, at speeds 0–1.6; over a cycle at every speed, no foot goes 2 cm below the ground and one is always within 2 cm of it; arms swing opposite the legs, wider with speed, and the pelvis bobs; step aside and hop stay finite and above ground, start standing, hop clears the ground, the step reaches out and looks at the car; eras dress differently; posing (walk, step aside, hop, blend, pose) allocates nothing.
- Sitting is tested in `figure.rs` too: the feet stay on the ground from standing to seated, the pelvis lands on the seat, and `phase_rate` matches the skeleton's.

## As built: cars (`traffic::Traffic`, `traffic::cars`)
Simulation only; drawing, the density setting and sound are in the app (below). `Traffic::new(&graph, TrafficBudget, seed)` builds a fixed pool (default 96 cars) and precomputes which turns conflict; nothing allocates after it (clones keep the reserved pool). Each 120 Hz tick the caller runs `step(&graph, &inputs)` then `populate(&graph, &inputs)`; `Inputs` carries the signal tick, the player (position, heading, speed, half extents), the camera as a horizontal view cone, the clock (seconds of day, dusk and dawn as the streetlights switch) and the density. `cars()` is the live set as a plain slice of `Car`: id (stable for its life), ground position under the middle, heading (vehicle convention) and unit direction, speed, shape index (below `TrafficBudget::shapes`), paint seed, head lights; then the sim state (lane or turn, front-bumper distance `s`, chosen turn, claims).
- **Kinematic, along the graph.** A car is a front-bumper distance on a lane or a turn (`seg`: a lane id, or the lane count plus a turn id), 4.6 × 1.8 m. Its pose is the midpoint of its front and rear points on the path (the rear may still be on the previous segment), heading from rear to front, so it swings through turns.
- **Following:** the intelligent-driver model (1.4 m/s² up, 2.5 comfortable, 8 emergency; 2 m jam gap; time gap 1.1–1.6 s per driver) against the nearest obstacle along the path it will drive (current lane or turn, the chosen turn, the lane after, up to 40–90 m ahead by speed): the car ahead, a closed stop line, or the player found within a car's width of the path. Desired speed is the lane's posted speed (its road class) × 0.9–1.1 per driver; turns cap it at √(2.2 m/s² × min radius), at least 3 m/s, and cars brake ahead for them. A hard limit under the model: a step never moves a car's front closer than 0.3 m to the car ahead (0.6 m to the player) or past a closed line. Under that a backstop: a move that would put a car's body in contact with another car's or the player's, or deeper into one, is not made; roads that cross without a junction (a few in the lakes and suburb places) are what it catches. The player is never hit by construction: a car stopping dead in front of a queue stops it, and cars only ever stand behind.
- **Junctions by claims.** A car must claim its turn to leave its lane, and claims it within its comfortable stopping distance plus 3 m when the gate is open: green (only if it reaches the line 0.5 s before red, gathering speed at half the maximum), yellow when it is too close to stop and still makes it; a stop sign stopped at (under 0.3 m/s within 2.5 m) and waited out (0.5–1.5 s, seeded); room past the junction for its length; no conflicting turn claimed; nobody with the right of way about to arrive; the player's footprint, or where the player will drive in the next 3 s, not on the turn (a player waiting at a red holds up nobody on the green). A car claims only a turn from its own lane. A claim is held through the turn and until the rear has left it; a car still on its lane gives it back on red or when held up out of its commit distance (a car ahead), and stops at the line, so no car enters on red. Two turns conflict when what a car sweeps on them (the curve and the first 5.6 m of the lane after) comes within 2.2 m of the other's or they merge into one lane; turns of nearby junctions are compared too. Turns from one lane share their mouth: cars on them follow each other there and conflict only where the curves run close beyond it.
- **Right of way** among conflicting cars within 5 s of their lines (head of each approach): uncontrolled and green before stop signs; straight and right before left and U-turns; then the earlier arrival (stop signs: the earlier stop); then the lower id. A strict order, so no two cars wait on each other. Red approaches do not contend.
- **Turn choice** when a car enters a turn (or spawns): over the turns from every lane of the road and direction it lands on, weighted straight 6, left or right 2, U-turn 0.25, twice for its own lane. A turn from a parallel lane is reached by changing lanes; a car that has not made it by 60 m from the end (or 10 m before it would commit, if further) picks again from its own lane. Lanes with no turns (one-way dead ends) end at a closed line.
- **Lane changes** (parallel lanes of one road and direction): to the lane the turn leaves from, or to pass a car (or the player) slower than 85% of its desired speed within 40 m when going straight. A car considers one every 0.25 s, at most one changes per step, never near the lane's end (as for picking again) or while it holds a claim; it needs 3 m + 0.6 s of speed ahead and behind on the target lane (more when closing) and the player not on it nearby. Its new position is the nearest point on the target lane; the drawn car stays put and the offset eases out at 1.4 m/s, and while it lasts the car still blocks the lane it left.
- **Population.** `Density` off/low/normal/busy keeps 0, ¼, ½ or all of the pool. Spawns: at most one per step, scanning 48 lanes per step round the graph for a point 150–300 m from the player, on a lane flowing toward the player, out of the view cone (with a car's margin), 12 m clear of every car and 25 m of any on its lane, at up to 80% of the posted speed and no faster than it can stop for what is ahead. Despawns, only out of view: beyond 360 m, hidden for 15 s and over 75 m away, stuck (stopped 90 s, longer than any red), or over the density target (beyond 150 m). Head lights come on 0–15 min after dusk per driver and go off as long after dawn.
- **Hit by the player** (`Traffic::shove`, from the app's collision, below): the change of velocity along the car's way that keeps it at or above standstill goes into its speed on its path, which the car ahead and the stop line still limit as ever; the rest slides it off its path (`Car::slide`, dying at 8 m/s², into `Car::shove`, a ground offset of the drawn car), and the yaw rate turns it (`Car::spin`, dying at 3 rad/s², into `Car::yaw`). A knock of 0.5 m/s or more has it brake at the emergency 8 m/s² to a stop and stand 1–2 s (`Car::hold`, the driver's share from its id); a lighter touch only nudges it. Then it drives on and steers back: the offset eases out by 0.15 m per meter driven, the yaw with it in proportion, so it never crabs and never snaps; standing, it stays where it was shoved. Every move of a shoved car goes through the same contact backstop, so a slide stops against another car or the player. Cars behind see it as any car ahead and queue. Only a car the player has hit has these fields set, and only then do they enter `Traffic::hash`.
- **Determinism:** one PCG stream (`skywave_core::Rng`, keyed by the seed) drawn in a fixed order; only IEEE add, multiply, divide and the crate's own `sqrt`/`atan2`/`sin_cos`; cars planned and moved in pool order. `Traffic::hash` is every car, bit for bit.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/tests/cars.rs`): a 12-car platoon on a straight lane settles to at least 2 m + 0.9 s of speed with no contact over 60 s; on a signalized crossing every car that reaches the line on red stops, every car goes through on green, none on red, and the crossing streams never touch; the player stopping dead ahead of six cars on a two-lane road is never touched and every car passes in the other lane; a player waiting at a red does not stop the green cross traffic; a claim held up behind a car that appears ahead is given back before red and the cross traffic goes on its green; busy traffic on the coast at seed 1 for 120 s (camera turning): nobody enters a junction on red, no two cars on one lane or turn closer than a car length, no contact between cars or with the player standing on a lane, no car appears or vanishes in view, no car moves further than its speed allows or turns more than 0.1 rad in a step, 94–96 cars of 96; every region at seeds 1–3 drives a minute busy with no contact, no jump and no red run; a car running at 10 m/s with a car behind, shoved forward and sideways with a little yaw, stops within 2 s, stands 1–2 s, drives on and is back on its lane line 4 s after the hit (at most 0.15 m off it, 0.03 rad of yaw), moving continuously and never nearer than 8 m to the car behind; the real vehicle sim rear-ending the last of two cars at a red at 20 km/h through `world::collide::hit_car` shoves it 0.69 m without touching the car ahead, it is off its brakes 1.5 s after and goes through on the green back on its lane line, and the bodies never overlap; the player standing across a one-lane road holds a queue of five whose head stops 2.9 m short of it, nobody touching or passing; low and normal hold 90% of their targets after 30 s, off spawns nothing; pinned `Traffic::hash` after 30 s on the coast at seed 1 (moves in its own commit, with the reason); no allocation in 20 s of stepping; 96 cars step and populate in under 0.5 ms p99 in a debug build (about 0.3 ms measured; asserted under `SKYWAVE_ENFORCE_BUDGETS`, the best of three 5 s windows, with the other tests in the file held off).

## As built: the sidewalk graph (`traffic::walks`)
Built once from a place and its lane graph (`WalkGraph::new(&place, &lanes)`, a pure function of both); `Place::sidewalks` is empty from every producer today, so like the lanes it is derived, on the geometry `world::roads` draws (its corner layout is mirrored, not shared: `traffic` does not depend on `world`).
- **Walks**: every road with sidewalks gets a walk line along each side, `WALK_LINE_M` (1.7 m) out from the curb face, on the slab past any parkway grass, following `world`'s layout centerline (straight from each node out to its trim, then the profile, mitered). A walk starts where that side's curb starts at the junction: the walks of two roads meeting there join around the corner between them, along an arc about the curb return's center (radius 4.5 m between locals, 6 m otherwise, less the walk offset) where the curbs turn in, through the point where the two walk lines meet on the outside of a bend, straight on where the roads continue each other (within 5°). A corner joins only two roads that both have sidewalks; nodes where two roads meet within 30° (merges) get no corners or crossings and their walks stop 20 m back. A walk cut back further than its edge is long (a stub, often where a chunk border splits a road just past a junction) carries the rest of the cut on through the straight two-road node at its far end into the next edge, and the corner joins that edge's walk, so corners stay clear of the junction's pavement however short the roads into it. Where two roads overlap and `world` lays one's pavement across the other's sidewalk, walk links running over another road (within its half width plus 0.3 m of its centerline, at the same level) are dropped, and so are walk links coming within 1 m of any lane's painted band (where the lane graph's lanes and the drawn road disagree near a junction): the walk ends there. Walks are cut into links of at most 16 m; a link knows its road edge, which side the road is on and its lateral room (1.45 m toward the curb, 0.75 m toward the back of the walk).
- **Crossings** go where `world` paints a crosswalk: at each end of a road with sidewalks meeting two or more others (not a highway, at least 12 m of road between its trims), 2.3 m past its trim. A crossing is a chain: a spur from the walk line out to the curb, then down onto the road, across it and back up (`LinkKind::Crossing`, the only links on asphalt), and a spur back. Pedestrians wait at the curb vertex: at least 0.35 m in from the curb face (just behind the curb top) and at least 0.8 m outside every lane's painted band as the lane graph lays it, pushed back along the spur as far as that takes. A group goes out along a spur and waits there in single file, 0.6 m apart, the first at the curb vertex, never past it. On a signalized junction (the lane graph's `Control::Signal` on a lane arriving on that road) `WalkGraph::walk` shows *walk* while that approach is red now and still red when someone starting now at 1.2 m/s would be across, plus 2 s (red is one unbroken stretch of the cycle, so two samples settle it); a crossing of a one-way road leaving the junction, or of an unsignalized one, is a look for a gap.
- **Spots**: shop windows (walls of commercial buildings facing a walk within 4.5 m; up to three per wall), doors (office, hotel, civic), parking-structure entrances, benches by a walk (two seats; on collectors and arterials also a stop to wait at: the place has no bus stops yet, so benches stand in for them), beach behind a walk (about every 40 m where the zone 6 m behind the walk is beach) and landmarks (the place's POIs and piers, at their nearest walk within 60 m). Each spot is a point on a link with a facing.
- **Density**: each link carries people per 100 m at normal density from its zone: Downtown and Strip 3, Beach 2.5, Commercial 2, Park 1.5, Residential 0.6, Suburb and Industrial 0.3, Hills 0.1, Farmland none.
- A 32 m grid lists the links and spots per cell for spawning and target picking. Coast seed 1: 12,305 nodes, 12,566 links, 57.2 km of walk, 558 crossings (215 signalized), 1,065 spots, about 1 MiB.

## As built: pedestrians (`traffic::peds`)
`Pedestrians::new(&walks, seed, era, budget)` allocates fixed pools from a `PedestrianBudget` (`SMALL` 128 people for web and Deck, `LARGE` 256); `step(&walks, &lanes, &PedInput)` runs one 120 Hz step from the signal clock's tick, the density setting, the camera's view cone and the player's car (the same `Density`, `View` and `Player` the NPC cars take; `Player::ABSENT` for none) and allocates nothing. One seeded PCG stream (`skywave_core::Rng`) drawn in a fixed order: the same seed and inputs give bit-identical people.
- **Walkers** are one person or a group of two or three (55/33/12%) that keeps together: a place on the graph (link, distance, direction), the slowest member's own pace (1.2–1.6 m/s each), and what it is doing (walking, waiting at a curb, crossing, holding for the car, hurrying off a crossing, staying at a spot). Members walk abreast 0.65 m apart, the group a little right of the walk line, each easing toward their slot (at up to their speed plus 0.7 m/s), so link changes, stops and formation changes never jump.
- **Routing**: a walker heads for a spot (one of a few random spots up to ~100 m off with room for it) or, failing that, a walk point; at each fork it takes the way whose far end (past plain two-link nodes) lies nearest, with up to 4 m of randomness and a 4 m dislike of crossing; after 120 s it picks another goal. At the spot it stays: a window 3–9 s, a door 4–12 s, a parking entrance 5–15 s, a stop 15–50 s, the beach or a landmark 20–60 s (in a ring of two or three facing in), a bench 20–80 s: two sit (walk to the seat, turn, `sit`), a third stands by. Benches, windows and doors take one walker at a time; other spots three, standing along from each other.
- **Crossings**: at the curb a walker looks 0.5–1.7 s, then goes on the walk phase (signals) or straight away (elsewhere), and in either case only while the player's car is neither on the crossing (within 1.5 m of it) nor coming toward it. On the road it never stops or holds.
- **The car** (nobody is collidable), at any speed including standing still: its danger zone is its footprint plus a margin and, while it moves, its path ahead (4 m + 2 s of its speed, its half width plus the margin to either side; ahead of the front of a car faster than 3 m/s the path widens as a cone, up to 0.2 m per meter at 6 m/s and at most 1 m, for a turn it may yet start). The path follows the car's turn: its yaw rate, taken from its heading the step before, gives a curvature, and the path is the circle it is driving on (straight below 0.01/m), widened by how far the body's corners swing out on it. A person within 0.4 m of that zone (room for a hand or a foot: the figure's limbs reach past where it stands) steps to the nearest spot within 2 m on the walk geometry around them (walks, corners and spurs, each within its width; on a crossing, its links too) that is clear of the car's footprint and of its whole path ahead by 0.4 m (a spot farther along the path is no way out), reachable in a straight line that never goes into the footprint (from inside it, only ever farther out), out of a moving car's way, if they start in it, before the car gets to them (at 2.5 m/s), and wherever the line there goes deeper into its way than where they stand (toward the middle of its path or across it), past that point with 0.8 s to spare before the car gets there. No spot is in a lane's painted band (`WalkGraph::in_lane`: near a junction the lane graph's lanes and a walk's edge can overlap), and from a crossing none is reached through a lane off the crosswalk. Only for a car on a walk itself (its footprint over the walk's band) is the gutter beside that walk (up to 0.4 m off the curb, short of the lanes) a spot too, and around a corner 0.3 m past the corner's room on either side (still on the sidewalk). With no such spot they still move: to the least bad spot there, the one clearest of the car's way that never goes into the footprint, preferring one past the car's way before it gets there, then one not through a lane; any side will do when none is. The move is a step aside (`step_aside`, up to about 0.7 m when the car is more than 0.8 s off) or a hop; with the car under 0.8 s off, or for a move longer than the hop's own 0.6 m, it is a startled jump, under way at once. Each move's duration keeps its curve's steepest part at 4 m/s at most: nobody ever moves faster. A move keeps going while its spot stays clear and changes course, once it has run 0.25 s, if the car turns into it. A walker on a crossing's road links hurries (2.2 m/s) along the crossing away from the car's path (away from a stopped car's middle), turning back if that is behind it, and keeps going while the way ahead is blocked; a car coming down the crosswalk itself makes people step aside across it instead while the walker waits, and so does a car the walker's way leads into (someone the walk back to their slot would take into the car steps out of its way across the crossing or back to a curb). On a crossing and the spur off it, and anywhere while someone stands in a lane or their way back to the group crosses one, a walker waits for anyone more than 1 m from their place in the group, so nobody is drawn on behind it diagonally across a lane (on the walk otherwise it goes on: a car that blocks someone for good must not hold a group for good); someone off a crossing's band while their group is on it, or in a lane off the crossings, walks straight back into the band of the group's link first; someone that far behind whom the car keeps from walking back steps out of its way as if it threatened. A walker waiting at the curb escapes too. Walkers stop 0.5 m (plus 1 m ahead) short of the zone and do not walk toward a moving car's path within a wider watched zone; after 4 s held by a stopped or creeping car they turn back (after 12 s for any car). Nobody eases into the zone, on the walk or on a crossing: the walk back to a slot waits until it is clear. Someone on a bench the car comes at gets up and steps aside. Once nothing has threatened for 0.6 s people walk back to their slots and carry on. Walking groups keep within 0.75 m of the walk line; the rest of the walk's width is for stepping aside. NPC cars do not see pedestrians yet (they only meet at crossings on the walk phase); that is for M4 #6/#8.
- **Density**: every 0.5 s the target is the zones' people per 100 m over the walks within 120 m of the eye, times 0, 0.35, 1 or 2 (off, low, normal, busy), capped by the budget. Every 50 ms, while under target, one spawn is tried 40–120 m away, out of view (with 3 m of room for the group's slots) and clear of the car: on a walk, weighted by its density, or (30%) already at a spot, seated on a bench or standing about. Nobody vanishes in view (the same cone test as spawning, `View::sees`): walkers out of view leave beyond 150 m, or one at a time beyond 40 m while more than two over target; with density off everyone leaves as soon as they are out of view.
- **Output**: per person `pos` (the figure's origin; the seat point while seated), `heading` (the vehicle sim's convention), `seed` (for `figure(seed, era)`), and `PedPose`: the gait's phase and speed, the action (step aside, hop, sit), its progress, side, look and blend weight; `PedPose::weights()` gives the gait, step aside, hop and sit weights and `PedPose::joints(&skeleton)` the blended `Joints`.
- **Tests** (`crates/traffic/tests/peds.rs`, seed 1 unless noted): the walks of all three regions lie off asphalt (99.8–99.9% of samples; the rest is where `world`'s junction pavement overlaps a sidewalk) and crossings on it (89%), every crossing's curbs join its spur and road, rebuilding gives the same graph. On the coast around the busiest stretch:
  - over 90 s busy, every person off a crossing is out of the lanes: never both within 0.5 m of a lane's band (its painted width from the lane graph) and on asphalt by the world's collider (near junctions where a road bends into its node the lane graph's lanes and the world's straightened road ends differ by up to a meter, so the band alone flags people on the curb; turn curves are left out, a few tight ones cut over the curb return), and within 2 m of a walk line; people on crossings within 1.6 m of them;
  - over 150 s every step onto a signalized crossing's road happens on red for that road (67 on walk, 135 at unsignalized crossings);
  - cars driven from behind at 24 people at 6–18 m/s get all 24 to step aside or hop, and nobody is ever inside the car's footprint (but anyone the car appears beside within its warning reach at its first step); the same for cars driven down the road through people mid-crossing;
  - with the camera on the car, zero person-steps inside its footprint with it stopped on a crosswalk for 60 s, creeping over one at 0.25 m/s, parked on the sidewalk after a 2.5 m/s approach, and driving 1–3 m/s through a crowd for 60 s with at least five people coming within 5 m of it (anyone it appears on at the first step gets 1.5 s to get out); during a 1 m/s drive along the busiest walk (the test finds it from where the people are) every person stepping aside stays within 1.5 m of a walk line (at most 1.2 m);
  - coast seeds 1–4, each built fresh, 60 s busy with the car (and the camera) driving 4–8 m/s along the busiest walk: nobody inside the car; nobody off a crossing in a lane's band on asphalt, nor within 0.5 m of one but in the gutter for 5 s after stepping aside; every move out of the way taking nobody more than 1.5 m from a walk line, or into the gutter beside it (1.9 m); everyone finite;
  - coast seeds 1–4, the two crossings with the most people near them (every crossing with anyone within 25 m with `SKYWAVE_ALL_CROSSINGS=1`) and the busiest walk, the car (and the camera) scripted through what a plain drive does not do: along each crosswalk from 12 m before one curb to 12 m past the other at 1, 2 and 3 m/s, forward and reversing; along it stopping on it for 6 s and driving on; turning across it both ways at 3 and 6 m/s on 6 and 9 m radii after 12 m straight in; appearing on a curb queue and standing there; standing on a sidewalk 2.4 m short of someone and pulling away at them; parking on the sidewalk after a 2.5 m/s approach (136 runs, 23 s alone in a debug build here and up to 2 min on a busier machine; 2152 runs with every crossing, about 6 min here): nobody ever inside it (but anyone it appears on at its first step, within 0.5 m of it or of where its front will be in 0.5 s, for 1.5 s), nobody in a lane's band on the asphalt off a crosswalk's band (by where they stand), every dodge on the sidewalk within the walks' bands (each walk's room either side of its line, give or take 0.15 m where the bands meet; out of them only as far as the move went for someone at a shop window; plus the 0.4 m gutter with the car on their walk; on the asphalt the lane check stands), no single move carrying anyone more than 2 m and nobody faster than 4 m/s;
  - groups stay within 3 m (widest 1.3 m); density bands hold within 20% (target 20/57/115 for low/normal/busy), off spawns nothing, nobody spawns in view or outside 40–120 m, nobody vanishes in view, and nobody is past 150 m unless someone of their group is in view (a group leaves together);
  - the pinned `Pedestrians::hash` after 30 s at seed 1 (moves only on purpose, in its own commit); stepping allocates nothing; and 128 people step, with the car driving through them, at a median under 0.5 ms and a p99 under 5 ms in a debug build behind `SKYWAVE_ENFORCE_BUDGETS` (as the cars: the best of three 5 s windows, each printed; median 0.15 ms, p99 0.21–0.26 ms measured here, p99 up to 0.46 ms on a busier machine).

## As built: traffic in the game (`app::npc`)
The app builds the lane graph and a `Traffic` (96-car pool, the first fourteen built-in shapes, `vehicles::TRAFFIC_SHAPES`: the everyday cars and the two common sports cars, not the exotics or the open sports cars) for the scene's place at setup, so a vehicle switch keeps it and the WebGL2 restart (a fresh `App::from_setup`) builds it again. Its density is **Settings › World › Traffic: Off / Low / Normal / Busy** (saved as `"world": { "traffic": "normal" }`, Normal by default), over which the scene key `traffic=` wins for the session; a `still=1` scene defaults to off, so the screenshot goldens and the self-test (`App::new` leaves traffic off too) draw and hash as before. Off drops every car at once and the sim stays idle. The same setting drives the pedestrians (below).
- **Step.** Each 120 Hz sim step, after the player's car and before the traffic: the collision (`Npcs::collide`): every NPC car within reach is met with the player's car through `world::collide::hit_car` (`docs/VEHICLES.md` item 6), each as its shape's body box (`npc::CarBody`: the shape's length and widest point, centered where the parked-car mesh puts the body round the car's ground point, and its car's mass), and a car hit while closing is shoved (`Traffic::shove`); the hardest hit joins the step's `world::Impact`, so the bump plays through the mixer by closing speed and the body jolts the camera as on a wall. Then the player (its pose, speed and half length and width, so the traffic sees the car switched to) and the camera's horizontal view cone (its field of view widened 15%, the last frame's aspect), the clock (head lights from 25 min after sunset to 25 min before sunrise, about when the photocell switches), then `step` and `populate`. The poses before the step are kept, and drawing interpolates between them by car id. With traffic on, `App::state_hash` includes the traffic's (the signal tick and every car).
- **Warm-up.** Whenever traffic is switched on, 20 s of it runs with the player and camera held still, so the streets round the car already have cars: all of it while the scene loads (`App::from_setup` for a scene's `traffic=`, `App::set_settings_store` for the saved setting), before the first frame; switched on from Settings in game, 120 steps a frame (a second of sim, about 10 ms of CPU) for 20 frames, instead of all 2400 in one.
- **Drawing.** Each car is one instance of a parked-car mesh (`car::parked_mesh`: body, glass, lamps and wheels in one) of its shape in one of the place's four era street colors for that shape (`propinst::place_paints`, the same paints the parked cars use; the car's paint seed picks one), level of detail 1 within 80 m and 2 out to 280 m, sorted by mesh id so each is one instanced draw. The meshes are the parked cars' wherever the place parks that shape and paint (on the coast at seed 1, every one: the traffic adds no mesh), and are built the first time traffic is on only where it does not (a session with traffic off never builds any; the most the traffic can add is 96 meshes, in a place that parks no cars at all). Parked-car meshes are built with their lamps lit and drawn with glow 0; an NPC car's glow is the car-lamp glow while its lights are on, so its head and tail lamps glow at night. A car with `traffic::Car::braking` set (slowing faster than 0.6 m/s² in the last step, and after that while it keeps slowing faster than 0.2 m/s² or creeps under 1 m/s in a queue or at a line, until it speeds up faster than 0.3 m/s²; and whenever it stands under 0.05 m/s and is not pulling away) adds an instance of its shape's brake overlay (`car::brake_overlay_mesh`) at the same level of detail, glowing with the car-lamp glow day or night and casting no shadow: one more instanced draw per shape and level with a braking car in view, a few dozen triangles each.
- **Lights.** The nearest 8 cars with their lights on within 150 m of the eye add their two low beams (the player's headlamp spot, from the shape's head lamps, with its corona) to the frame's dynamic lights after the player's car's: at most 16 of the 256, nearest first; the static lights fill the rest. In each instance's 8 light slots the beams rank below every other light (`lighting::light_set_ranked`): a beam takes a slot only when the player's lamps and the street lamps reaching the instance all have theirs, so busy traffic never makes them drop out or flicker.
- **Sound.** The nearest four moving cars within 120 m are the world synth's traffic voices (`audio::synth::TrafficVoice`): a low-passed pulse at a firing frequency of 30 Hz + 4.5 Hz per m/s, shifted by the Doppler factor of the closing speed between the car and the player's car, panned by bearing from the camera and attenuated as 6 m over the distance. A car keeps its voice while it stays among the nearest four, so no pitch jumps when two swap rank. No horns.
- **Overlay.** F3 shows the live cars and the traffic sim's time per step (`docs/images/traffic-overlay.png`: busy traffic at a coast junction in WebGL2 on SwiftShader; the browser's timer rounds the step time to 0.1 ms).
- **Budgets.** The in-game step and populate of busy traffic on the coast: median under 0.5 ms and p99 under 2 ms a step in a debug build (best of three 600-step windows, asserted under `SKYWAVE_ENFORCE_BUDGETS`). At the junction of the `coast-traffic-noon` golden (busy, with queued, oncoming and crossing cars in view), Deck and Medium stay within the draws and triangles in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` (`platform-native` `traffic_at_a_junction_within_budget`, on the no-op device, in CI). The parked and NPC car meshes together stay within the parked-car memory budget.
- **Tests** (`crates/app/src/npc.rs`, `crates/app/tests/traffic.rs`): scene setup builds and warms up traffic for the town and the coast; one instance per live car in reach, in mesh order, both levels of detail; off draws nothing, drops every car and idles the sim; at night the beams stay within their share of the lights, nearest first, spots with coronas, the lamps glow, none by day; NPC beams never crowd out another light in any light set at a night junction, and the player's car keeps its own beams; voices are pitched up approaching and down going away, panned to their side, and keep their slot by car id; switched on in game the warm-up runs at most a batch a frame; 10 s of busy traffic at night stepped and drawn every frame allocates nothing (allocation counter); the density setting round-trips through the settings file.
- **Gate scenarios** (`crates/app/src/npc/gate.rs`, the M4 gate, results in `docs/MILESTONES.md`): the player driven by input through the app on the coast: 60 mph through a busy signalized junction against the light (no NPC car ever overlaps the player's footprint); a minute stopped on a downtown crosswalk and nosing slowly into the people on a corner, with a `traffic::Pedestrians` stepped beside the app on the app's player and camera (nobody ever inside the car; step aside and hop fire); reversing at 1.5 m/s into a queue standing at a red (it bumps the first car of the queue and stops 0.05 m short of where the bodies would meet; that car is shoved 0.22 m, brakes and lights its brake lamps; no NPC body ever overlaps the player's, each measured as its shape's box); a minute of random bumping at busy traffic round a junction (steering at the nearest car with a random pedal, forward or reverse: 147 hits, everything finite, no hit adds more than the rebound to the player's speed, the body never more than 0.2 m above where it started); Low/Normal/Busy hold non-overlapping car-count bands for 120 s at three places, and Off never steps the sim.
- **Not yet.** NPC wheels do not turn.

## As built: pedestrians in the game (`app::peds`)
`npc::Npcs` owns the place's pedestrians beside its traffic: at scene setup it builds the sidewalk graph (`WalkGraph::new` over the lane graph) and a `Pedestrians` pool of `PedestrianBudget::SMALL` (128 people, on web, Deck and desktop alike: the busy target on the coast's busiest block is about 115), with the traffic's lifetimes (a vehicle switch keeps them, the WebGL2 restart builds them again). One density for both: **Settings › World › Traffic** (and the scene's `traffic=`) sets the cars' and the people's, Off / Low / Normal / Busy, because the sim takes the same `Density` and the pools are fixed apart from it; a separate item would only be a second switch for the same thing. So `still=1` scenes, the goldens without `traffic=` and the self-test have no people. Off sends everyone home at once and idles the sim.
- **Step.** Each 120 Hz step, after the traffic's: the signal tick the traffic reached, the density, the camera's view cone and the player's car (`npc::player`, the traffic's own), then `step`. Every slot's person before the step is kept, so drawing interpolates position, heading, gait phase (the short way round the cycle), speed and the action's progress and weight by person id; a new person, a new action (sitting moves the origin to the seat) or a jump over 1 m draws as it is now. The player's car is the traffic's but with its signed speed, so a reversing car's danger zone is behind it. With pedestrians on, `App::state_hash` includes theirs.
- **Warm-up.** With the traffic's 20 s (all of it while the scene loads, else a batch a frame), and while the scene loads 40 s of people alone before it, with nobody watching (the view cone narrowed to a line ahead of the camera), so the first frame already has people walking near the camera, not only 40 m out where they appear. Those 40 s run at signal ticks a whole number of every junction's cycle past tick 0 (the lights are the same), so the signals and the cars warm up exactly as without people.
- **Figures.** The first time the density is on, eight figures of the place's era are built (`figure(seed, era)`, the seeds hashed from the place's, each the first wearing the next outfit in turn so the crowd wears all five) and kept: 13 part meshes each, 104 meshes, about 8.3k triangles and 0.27 MB on the coast (budget 3 MB, `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`). A person is drawn as the figure its seed picks (`seed % 8`), scaled to the height the sim gave it, so the stride the sim paces matches the drawn one.
- **Drawing.** Every live person within 150 m of the eye is 13 instances, one per part, posed by `PedPose::joints` and `Skeleton::pose` (walk, step aside, hop and sit blended by the sim's weights), sorted by figure and part so each part mesh is one instanced draw. Nearer than 40 m they cast shadows (farther, a shadow is a few texels of the far cascade and not worth the draws); nearer than 160 m the frame's lights light them. Pedestrians add no lights.
- **Overlay.** F3 shows the live people and the pedestrian sim's time per step (`docs/images/peds-overlay.png`: `peds 120 people  step 0.041 ms` on the busiest coast block in WebGL2 on SwiftShader).
- **Budgets.** The in-game pedestrian step (busy, the coast's busiest block): median under 0.5 ms a step in a debug build (best of three 600-step windows, asserted under `SKYWAVE_ENFORCE_BUDGETS`; 0.13 ms measured). The junction of the `coast-traffic-noon` golden with busy traffic and people: Deck and Medium within the ceilings in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`.
- **Tests** (`crates/app/src/peds.rs`, `crates/app/tests/traffic.rs`): scene setup builds the sidewalk graph, the figures and a warmed-up crowd on the coast, and a vehicle switch keeps it; the Traffic setting sets the pedestrian density at every level and round-trips through the settings file; every live person in reach is drawn as all 13 parts, in mesh order, over 240 steps with no allocation; off draws nobody, empties the pool and idles the sim, and on again everyone is drawn at their own height; driving 2 m/s forward and 5 m/s in reverse for 60 s each up and down 60 m of walk through the thickest crowd on the busiest block, through `App::update` and `App::scene_view`, nobody's origin, pelvis, torso or head is ever inside the car's footprint (hands and feet at most 1 cm, asserted under 5 cm), people step aside and hop, and every part of every figure stays within its bounds (no exploded figures); 10 s of busy traffic and people at night stepped and drawn every frame allocates nothing; the figures' meshes stay within their budget; the step budget above.
- **Golden.** `coast-peds-noon`: busy people on the coast's busiest block at noon from the mid chase camera.
